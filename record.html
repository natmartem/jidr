Thank you for helping!

  1. Tap the red button, say the word once, tap again to stop. It plays back so you can check it.
  2. Read exactly what is written, with the vowel marks, in Standard Arabic (fuṣḥā), at a normal pace.
  3. For letters, say the sound shown (for example “ba”), not the letter's name.
  4. Not happy? Just record again. Everything is saved on this phone as you go, so you can stop and come back later.
  5. When you're done (or done for today), tap Send recordings at the bottom and send the file to Natalia.

Your recordings stay yours. Natalia uses them only inside her learning app. They are not covered by the app's open licenses, and she won't reuse them elsewhere without asking you.
